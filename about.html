About the Chamber

A working legislature for a family that takes its fun seriously — and a serious piece of engineering wearing a party hat.

Why a congress?

Because "whoever yells loudest picks the movie" was not scaling. The Cousin Congress gives family decisions the dignity they deserve: motions, debate, recorded votes, and a gavel (a wooden spoon, held with authority).

Every decision — from Pizza Friday to reunion sites — goes through the same pipeline real legislation does. Bills are drafted, referred, amended, and voted on the record. Grievances become amendments. Amendments become law. The archive remembers everything, which is very on-brand for this family.

The yeas and nays were demanded, and the pizza was ordered. — Journal of the First Cousin Congress, Sitting 9

Under the hood

A chamber with no single point of failure

Local-first

Every action is an operation appended to a log on your own device. Votes count the instant you cast them — on a plane, in a basement, wherever. The network is an optimization, never a requirement.

Peer-to-peer

Devices exchange their logs tab-to-tab, over an optional Cloudflare relay, and directly browser-to-browser over WebRTC. As long as one cousin is online, a new device can recover the entire record from scratch.

Conflict-free

Operations carry hybrid logical clocks and merge deterministically, so two devices editing offline converge on the same state when they meet — no lost votes, no merge dialogs, no arguing (about the data, anyway).

The standing rules

Rules of the chamber

Quorum

A majority of enrolled members, attending in person or by video, constitutes a quorum. The chamber may gossip without a quorum but may not legislate.

Recognition

Members speak when recognised by the Speaker. Interrupting with "actually" is disorderly conduct on the third offence.

Voting

Votes are cast yea, nay, or present. A ballot may be changed at any time before the chair closes the roll. The record shows only the final ballot.

Thresholds

Ordinary questions carry by simple majority of decisive ballots. Schedule extensions on school nights, rule suspensions, and anything involving the good couch require two-thirds. Renaming the family group chat requires unanimous consent, as history demands.

Proxies

A member may delegate their ballot to another member. The delegate's vote counts for the absent member on any question the absent member has not voted on personally. A member's own ballot always outranks their proxy.

Decorum

Members shall not impugn one another's snack preferences on the record. Off the record is a different matter and always has been.

The record

The record is append-only. Errors are corrected by subsequent entries, never by erasure. Yes, this rule is also how the software works. No, that is not a coincidence.

Questions

Frequently raised points of order

Do I need an account?

No. You claim a seat from the member roster on the device you use, and that device signs its entries. The chamber runs on trust and version vectors, roughly in that order.

What happens if I vote while offline?

Your ballot is recorded on your device immediately and replicated when you next meet any other device — a peer, a tab, or the relay. The tally everyone sees updates at that moment, credited at full value.

What if two devices disagree?

They can't, for long. All operations carry logical timestamps and merge in the same order everywhere. For a single member's ballot on a single question, the latest cast wins — the same rule the floor uses in person.

Can the record be lost?

Only if every replica is lost at once. Each device holds the full log, every pairing copies it, and the console can export it as a file. One surviving copy — device, export, or relay — rebuilds everything.

Is the Cloudflare Worker required?

No. It adds an always-on relay (so devices needn't be online simultaneously), durable off-device backup, and automatic peer pairing. The chamber is fully functional without it, including direct invite-code pairing.

Constituent services

Write to the Clerk

Petitions, grievances, topping proposals, and formal compliments are all received. Messages go to the clerk's office privately — they are the one thing that is not replicated to every cousin.

No relay configured? Your message waits safely on this device and delivers itself when the office reopens.
