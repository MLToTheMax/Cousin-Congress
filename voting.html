Rule 9 — proxies

Delegate your vote

Away for exams or a school trip? Hand your ballot to a trusted cousin. Their vote is counted as yours on any question you haven't voted on yourself, and you can revoke at any time.

Your own ballot, when you cast one, always outranks the proxy.

How thresholds work

  • Majority More yeas than nays among decisive ballots.
  • Two-thirds Schedule changes and rule suspensions.
  • Unanimous One nay sinks it. Use sparingly.
