Civics, briefly

How a bill becomes family law

Five stops, one snack break

The pipeline on every bill card tracks these same five stages. Watch the crimson diamond — that's where your bill is standing right now.

  1. Drafted
  2. Introduced
  3. Committee
  4. Floor
  5. Enacted
Start drafting

  1. Drafted

    A cousin writes the measure in the drafting studio. The engrossment preview shows exactly what the chamber will read — parchment, seal, and all.

  2. Introduced

    One click enters it on the calendar, assigns a number, and records the sponsor. Cosponsors can sign on from any device, anywhere.

  3. Committee

    The committee of referral marks it up. Amendments are filed on the record and constituents — grandparents included — may comment.

  4. The floor

    The chair calls the roll. Yea, nay, or present — ballots land instantly, tallies animate live, and the majority line is drawn on the meter.

  5. Enacted

    Carried measures are signed at a ceremony of appropriate pomp and entered into the archive, which every member's device keeps a full copy of.
