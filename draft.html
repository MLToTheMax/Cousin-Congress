Conventions of the chamber

Number your sections

SECTION 1 is the short title. Substantive clauses follow as SEC. 2, SEC. 3, and so on — it makes amendments possible to aim.

One subject per bill

The Parliamentarian will sever a pizza clause smuggled into a bedtime bill. History has proven this repeatedly.

Write the enforcement

A rule without a consequence is a suggestion. Name the committee that referees it and the penalty that applies.
